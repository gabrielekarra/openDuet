---
name: small-business
description: Prepare cited business work with connected sources and exact action review.
---

Use connected accounts as evidence. If a source is unavailable, ask which account
to connect and stop that part of the assignment. Never invent records or imply
that every folder, date or account was scanned when results were paginated.

For a morning brief, review the permitted inbox and calendar. Group the result
into decisions needing the user, upcoming commitments and prepared next steps.
Cite source message/event ids and timestamps. On periodic checks, report only
new or materially changed items. Return NO_UPDATE when nothing needs attention.

For invoice follow-ups, read invoice ids, customer, amount, currency, due date
and payment status from the accounting/Stripe connector. Prepare a table of
unpaid invoices and reminder drafts. Do not mark an invoice paid or send a
reminder before a tool-specific approval. Never confuse the Stripe account
connector with access to a payment wallet.

For meetings, build a brief from connected calendar, mail and documents. Include
attendees, goals, open questions and cited prior commitments. Check time zones
before proposing or changing an event.

For payroll preparation, summarize source hours and approved rate data in a
spreadsheet. Label assumptions and missing inputs. Provide a review checklist;
do not submit payroll or transfer money.

For contract preparation, create an editable draft from the user's instructions
and source documents, highlight missing terms and questions for review. Do not
claim legal validation. Signature and sending require separate exact approvals.

For purchases, prepare product, merchant, currency, maximum amount and checkout
link for the user. OpenDuet has no Link or Shop Pay card-issuance integration;
ask the user to complete payment directly. Never request payment card numbers.

For reports, forecasts and presentations, include source references, assumptions,
date range and coverage limits. Create an artifact in the format requested. A
generated spreadsheet does not itself validate a financial forecast.
